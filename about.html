The Institute Journey

A Trusted Sanctuary for Second Chances in Education

NIOS Career Point is a premier student guidance and educational consultancy centre based in Berhampur, dedicated to helping students build a strong academic and career foundation. Established in 2011, the institute has successfully guided more than 2,500 students in choosing the right educational path and achieving their academic goals.

We specialize in providing comprehensive support for Direct 10th (Secondary), +2 Senior Secondary (Arts, Science, Commerce), graduation, and post-graduation programs through recognized boards and reputed universities across India.

Our philosophy is simple: education should never be out of reach because of past examination failures, family hardships, or discontinued schooling. Every learner deserves an encouraging mentor who understands official government board guidelines and provides honest, step-by-step guidance.

Our Guiding Pillars

How We Support Every Student

1. Personalized Educational Counselling

We take the time to understand each student’s previous academic history, strengths, and schedule constraints, recommending only the most suitable courses and subject combinations.

2. Flawless Admission Assistance

From document collection and eligibility verification to online portal enrollment with NIOS or UGC-approved universities, we ensure complete accuracy.

3. End-to-End Academic Handholding

We provide printed study material guidance, assistance with mandatory Tutor Marked Assignments (TMAs), practical exam coordination, and hall ticket delivery until the final certificate is in hand.
