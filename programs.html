Academic Course Directory

Find the Right Path for Your Future.

Whether you need to complete your matriculation, clear intermediate streams for competitive exams, or attain a recognized university degree while working, our accredited programs ensure peace of mind and legal recognition across India.

Secondary Education • NIOS Board

Direct 10th (Secondary)

A recognized matriculation pathway designed specifically for students who failed in traditional school boards (CBSE, ICSE, BSE Odisha) or discontinued their education due to personal hurdles. Conducted under the National Institute of Open Schooling (Ministry of Education, Govt. of India).

✓
Transfer of Credit (TOC): Transfer marks for up to two passed subjects from previous recognized boards to save your academic year.
✓
Flexible Subject Selection: Choose from over 15 subjects including languages (Odia, English, Hindi), Data Entry, Science, and Social Science.
✓
End-to-End Centre Support: Admission verification, Tutor Marked Assignment (TMA) solutions, practical guidelines, and hall ticket delivery.

Senior Secondary • Arts, Science, Commerce

+2 Senior Secondary (Intermediate)

Equivalent to Class 12 of CBSE and State Intermediate Boards (CHSE Odisha). Valid for national competitive entrances including NEET, JEE, CUET, Defence services (NDA, Navy), and higher university degree admissions across India.

✓
Three Specialized Streams: Arts (History, Pol Science, Economics, Odia), Commerce (Accounts, Business Studies), and Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Math).
✓
Practical Lab Guidance: Complete assistance with lab manuals, practical record notebooks, and viva examination preparation.
✓
Dual Exam Cycles: Enrol for annual public exams (April-May / October-November) or flexible On-Demand Examination (ODE).

Undergraduate Degrees • UGC Recognized

Graduation Programs (UG)

Bachelor degree admissions conducted through premier UGC and DEB-recognized distance education universities. Tailored for working professionals, intermediate dropouts, and adult learners who require a flexible study pace to obtain a legal graduate degree.

✓
Degrees Offered: B.A (Bachelor of Arts), B.Com (Bachelor of Commerce), B.Sc (Bachelor of Science), B.C.A (Computer Applications), and B.B.A (Business Administration).
✓
Government & UPSC Validity: Legally valid for all state civil services (OPSC/OAS), UPSC Civil Services, Banking (IBPS/SBI), Railways, and SSC exams.
✓
Working-Friendly Pacing: Study materials provided directly; flexible assignment submission and term-end exam schedules.

Postgraduate Degrees • Master's Qualifications

Post Graduation Programs (PG)

Advanced postgraduate degrees for bachelor graduates aiming to qualify for promotional criteria, lectureship eligibility (UGC-NET/SLET), or higher career scaling across private and public sectors.

✓
Master's Degrees Offered: M.A (Odia, English, History, Pol Science), M.Com (Advanced Accounting & Finance), M.Sc (Maths, IT, Chemistry), M.B.A (HR, Finance, Marketing), and M.C.A.
✓
Academic & Career Gateway: Fulfills mandatory criteria for college lectureship exams (NET/SET) and PhD entrance qualifications.
✓
Convenient Blended Learning: Comprehensive examination assistance and documentation managed directly by our Berhampur office.
