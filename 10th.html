Direct 10th Pass Opportunity for Failed & Dropout Students

Did you fail in school board exams or drop out before completing your Matriculation? At NIOS Career Point Berhampur, we guide you to successfully complete Class 10 through a legally recognized Government of India board.

Board NIOS (Govt. of India)
Eligibility Failed 10th or Min. 14 Years Age
Recognition Equiv. to BSE Odisha / CBSE / ICSE
Validity Valid for +2 & All Govt. Jobs
A Legitimate Second Chance

Overcome Academic Setbacks with Confidence

Many students face challenges during formal schooling due to health, family difficulties, or academic pressure, leading to dropped years or failed board results. The National Institute of Open Schooling (NIOS), established under the Ministry of Education, Government of India, provides a flexible and dignified pathway to clear Class 10.

At NIOS Career Point Berhampur (Estd. 2011), we provide end-to-end support for students from Berhampur, Ganjam, and across Southern Odisha. You do not have to repeat an entire school year in a traditional classroom; you prepare with curated study materials and appear for examinations on convenient schedules.

Why Choose Direct 10th Through NIOS?

  • Opportunity for Failed Students: Re-appear only for failed subjects via Transfer of Credit (TOC) and save your academic year.
  • Ideal for School Dropouts: Resume education at any age with flexible self-paced learning.
  • Legally Recognized Certificate: Equivalent to CBSE, ICSE, and state boards for all admission and job purposes.
  • Flexible Subject Selection: Choose 5 subjects tailored to your strengths (languages, sciences, humanities, or vocational subjects).
  • Comprehensive Support: Complete assistance with online registration, study material, TMA assignment preparation, and practical exams.

Available 10th Secondary Subjects

Students must select a minimum of 5 subjects (including at least one language subject) to receive the secondary passing certificate.

Continuous Support

How NIOS Career Point Guides Your Success

1. Admission & Documentation

We verify your previous mark sheets, Aadhaar card, and photographs to ensure zero errors on the NIOS official admission portal.

2. Tutor Marked Assignments (TMA)

TMAs carry 20% weightage in your final score. We provide solved reference materials and handle digital uploads before board deadlines.

3. Practical Lab Guidance & Hall Tickets

We coordinate your Practical PCP sessions at accredited Berhampur centres and ensure your hall tickets are distributed promptly.
