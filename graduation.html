Academic Elevation

Re-ignite Your Career with a Genuine Bachelor's Degree

Without a recognized undergraduate degree, job promotions, higher civil service examinations, and government recruitment opportunities remain out of reach. Many young people in Berhampur and Ganjam had to leave regular college prematurely to earn a living or support their households.

At NIOS Career Point Berhampur (Estd. 2011), we partner with reputed, UGC-approved universities offering blended and distance degree programs. We provide end-to-end assistance: selecting the right university, completing enrollment, delivering syllabi, assisting with project submissions, and coordinating examination schedules that do not interrupt your employment.

Who Is Eligible to Apply?

  • Discontinued College Students: Candidates who completed +2 but dropped out during their 1st, 2nd, or 3rd year of college.
  • Working Professionals: Individuals currently employed in retail, administration, healthcare, or trades needing a degree for promotion.
  • Homemakers & Adult Learners: Women and mature candidates seeking to complete their education with flexibility.
  • Candidates Seeking Higher Entrances: Those aiming to qualify for UPSC, State PSC, Bank PO, SSC CGL, or MBA entrances.
