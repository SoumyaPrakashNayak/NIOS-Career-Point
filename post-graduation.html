Master’s Credentials Tailored Around Your Professional Life

Many working professionals and graduates find it challenging to attend daily university lectures while managing career and family commitments. At NIOS Career Point Berhampur (Estd. 2011), we provide specialized admission guidance into UGC-approved university postgraduate courses designed for remote and distance study.

Whether you need an MBA to accelerate corporate advancement, an M.A in humanities or language to appear for teaching qualifications, an M.Com to advance in financial management, or an MCA for high-tier IT positions, our counselors guide you to the university best matched to your budget and career goals.

Who Can Apply?

  • Graduates Seeking Upgrades: Any graduate wanting a master's credential to meet promotion or exam criteria.
  • Working Professionals: Individuals needing accredited postgraduate degrees with flexible examination windows.
  • Aspiring Lecturers & Educators: Candidates aiming to sit for UGC-NET, CSIR-NET, or State Eligibility Tests (SLET/SET).
  • Candidates who discontinued earlier PG programs: Complete your degree with structured academic support.
